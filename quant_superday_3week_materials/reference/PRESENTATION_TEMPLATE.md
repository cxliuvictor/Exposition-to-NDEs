# Research presentation template

## Slide 1 — Problem and data
- one sentence objective
- unit of observation
- target/action
- sample size and time span
- one critical data caveat

## Slide 2 — What the data told me
Use at most 1–2 figures.
- strongest relationship
- cleaning/leakage decision
- key transformation hypothesis

## Slide 3 — Methodology
- baseline
- main models
- validation split/CV
- metric(s) and why

## Slide 4 — Results
Compact model comparison table.
Show OOS/validation separately.
Do not lead with training fit.

## Slide 5 — Decision/strategy or robustness
- threshold/ranking/portfolio rule if relevant
- bucket/regime/time stability
- costs/turnover if relevant

## Slide 6 — Conclusion
- one main result
- one caveat
- one next experiment

### Verbal discipline
Say what you tried *and rejected*. Distinguish:
- observation from interpretation
- validation from test
- prediction accuracy from decision value
