# Closing 1-hour interview drill

After the full mock, spend 60 minutes without code.

## 15 min probability/statistics
- conditional expectation and Bayes
- LLN/CLT
- covariance/correlation
- regression assumptions
- bias/variance
- regularization
- hypothesis testing / confidence intervals
- bootstrap
- selection bias / multiple testing

## 15 min ML/data
- Ridge vs Lasso vs tree boosting
- cross-validation designs
- leakage
- missing values
- class imbalance
- calibration
- permutation importance vs SHAP
- feature transformations
- distribution shift

## 15 min finance/trading
- Sharpe
- realized vs implied vol
- delta hedging intuition
- PnL vs prediction metrics
- costs/turnover
- signal decay
- overfitting a backtest

## 15 min project defence
Pick one CV project and answer:
- problem
- your contribution
- validation
- overfitting controls
- failure mode
- what you would change now
