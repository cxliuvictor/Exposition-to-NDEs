# Week 1 mastery checklist

By the end of Week 1 you should do these without AI:
- pandas: filtering, groupby/agg/transform, merge/join/concat/cross, pivot/melt/explode, rolling/shift/rank/qcut
- NumPy: axes, broadcasting, masks, where/select, clipping, quantiles, argsort, correlation, vectorisation
- transformations: log, cyclic sin/cos, sigmoid/tanh, interactions, lags/rolling, smooth event proximity
- matplotlib: hist/scatter/time/subplots/correlation/actual-vs-predicted/savefig
- cleaning: constants, near constants, NaN/sentinels/inf, duplicates, IDs, leakage, outliers, shift
- sklearn: pipelines, scaling/imputation/one-hot, Ridge, Huber, logistic, tree/boosting
- validation: 80/10/10, KFold vs TimeSeriesSplit, cross-validation, frozen OOS test
- metrics: RMSE/MAE/R²; precision/recall/F1/ROC-AUC/PR-AUC; bucketed errors
- interpretation: permutation importance and conceptual SHAP
- strategy: baseline rule, signal alignment, threshold selection, costs/turnover, PnL/drawdown
- presentation: one clear result figure and a 5-minute research story
